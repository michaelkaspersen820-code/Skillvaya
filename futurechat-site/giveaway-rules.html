Future Chat monthly giveaway — rules

  1. Promoter: Future Chat (Skillvaya), South Africa. Contact: Help and safety → Report a problem in the app.
  2. Start: the giveaway starts on 1 December 2026. Only friends who join from that date count. The first draw is on 1 January 2027 for December 2026, then on the 1st of every month for the month before.
  3. Prize: the cash prize shown in the app for that month (R500 unless the app shows otherwise), paid by EFT into a South African bank account in the winner's own name within 30 days of the draw. It can't be exchanged for anything else.
  4. Free to enter: no payment or purchase is needed or possible. The only way to get a ticket is to invite a friend to Future Chat. You get 1 ticket for each friend who joins during that month through your invite link, your invite code or an invite sent to their phone number, saves their phone number in the app and sends at least 3 messages within 30 days of joining.
  5. Each friend counts once, for the first person who invited them. Fake, duplicate or bought accounts don't count, and anyone who tries to cheat can be disqualified.
  6. The draw: the app draws 1 winner at random from all tickets at 08:00 (South African time). More tickets give more chances. A record of every draw is kept for at least 3 years.
  7. Who can enter: anyone with a Future Chat account. Winners under 18 must have a parent or guardian accept the prize for them. The promoter, staff and anyone who helps run the giveaway, and their close family, can't win.
  8. The winner is told in Future Chat and by notification, and must reply within 14 days or a new winner is drawn. Their first name and the first letter of their surname are shown in Shout-outs in the app.
  9. Personal information is used only to run the giveaway, as set out in the Future Chat Privacy Policy.
  10. These rules are free to anyone who asks, including the National Consumer Commission. The promoter may change or end the giveaway with notice in the app; a month that has already started is completed.
