Future Chat Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026 · South Africa

Future Chat is a messaging and calling app run by Skillvaya (South Africa). This policy explains what information Future Chat uses, why, who it is shared with and the choices you have. It applies to the Future Chat app and to Future Chat inside the Skillvaya app, which use the same accounts.

1. Information you give us

  • Account: your name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), country and language.
  • Phone number: your mobile number, so friends and family can find you. To prove the number is yours, you send one normal SMS with a short code (for example "FC 123456") from your own phone to the Future Chat verification number. We see the number the SMS came from and the code; we do not read any other SMS on your phone. You pay your network's normal rate for that one SMS.
  • Profile: profile photo, "about" text and the settings you choose.
  • What you send: messages, photos, videos, voice notes, documents, statuses, polls, lists, reminders, events, check-ins and anything else you post in chats, groups and Circles.
  • Purchases: what you bought (for example a theme pack, Future Chat Plus, a shout-out or a Status ad) and the Google Play order reference. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card or bank details.
  • Ads and shout-outs: the words, picture or video you submit, who it is for and the audience you choose.

2. Information from your phone (only with your permission)

  • Contacts: if you allow it, the phone numbers in your address book are compared with Future Chat members so you can see who is already here. The numbers are compared for that request only and are not stored.
  • Location: only when you choose to share it — a location message, a family check-in ("I'm home safe"), SOS, or live location sharing you switch on. Shared locations are rounded and only go to the people or chat you picked. Future Chat does not track your location in the background.
  • Camera and microphone: for photos, videos, voice notes, Lens effects and calls. Face tracking for Lenses runs on your phone; your face data is not sent to us.
  • Notifications: a device token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can tell you about new messages and calls.
  • Install referrer: when you install from someone's invite link, Google Play tells the app which invite code was used, so the friend who invited you is credited in the monthly giveaway.

3. Information created when you use Future Chat

  • Message delivery and read status, call history (who, when, how long — not the call itself), status views, and the time you were last active (you can hide this).
  • Basic technical information such as app version, device type and error reports, used to keep the service working and secure.

4. Calls

Voice and video calls connect directly between the phones where possible. When they can't, they may pass through a relay server that forwards the encrypted audio and video without recording it. We do not record calls.

5. How long we keep things

  • Photos, videos, voice notes and files are removed from our server once everyone in the chat has received them, and at the latest after 30 days. Your phone keeps its own copy.
  • Statuses disappear after 24 hours.
  • Messages stay until you or the chat clears them, or the account is deleted.
  • Purchase and giveaway records are kept for as long as the law requires (usually up to 5 years for financial records and 3 years for giveaway draws).
  • SMS verification codes expire after 30 minutes.

6. Why we use information

To run Future Chat (deliver messages and calls, show statuses, let people find you), keep it safe (stop fake accounts, spam and abuse, act on reports), provide what you bought, run the free monthly giveaway, show Sponsored statuses and shout-outs, answer support requests and meet legal duties. We use the minimum information needed for each purpose.

7. Ads

Sponsored statuses and shout-outs are bought by businesses and people inside Future Chat and are approved by us before they show. They are not chosen from your messages. We do not use advertising IDs and we do not sell your information to anyone.

8. Who we share with

  • The people you chat with see what you send them, your name, photo and number (you can limit some of this in Privacy settings).
  • Service providers who run parts of Future Chat for us: hosting and database (Render), push notifications (Google Firebase), payments (Google Play) and call relays. They may only use the information to provide that service.
  • The law: when we are legally required to, or to protect someone's safety.

Some of these providers may store information outside South Africa. When that happens we rely on their security and privacy commitments as required by the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

9. Children and teens

Future Chat is not meant for children under 13. Teens can use Teen safe mode, which limits who can add them to groups. If you think a child under 13 has an account, report it in the app and we will remove it.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Change your profile, privacy (who sees your last seen, profile photo, status and birthday) and notification settings in the app.
  • Block or report anyone from their chat.
  • Clear chats or back them up in Settings.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Account → Delete my account, or use the account deletion page. Your account, profile, messages, statuses and phone number are deleted; records we must keep by law are kept only as long as required.
  • Under POPIA (and GDPR or similar laws where you live) you may ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how it is used. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

11. Security

Connections to Future Chat are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as secure hashes, and access to stored information is limited. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong password and keep your phone locked.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.

13. Contact

Use Help and safety → Report a problem in the app, or email skillvaya@gmail.com.
