Account and privacy support

Request deletion of your Skillvaya account

You can request deletion of your Skillvaya account and associated personal information using the signed-in app or by emailing Skillvaya from the email address registered to your account.

For an in-app request: open Skillvaya, sign in, open Profile, scroll to the account actions and select Request account deletion. Follow the confirmation prompt. A request is recorded for review; it does not instantly delete the account.

Submit a request from this device

If you are signed in to Skillvaya on this browser or app, you can send a request directly. The request does not delete your account immediately; an owner must verify and process it.

Request by email

If you cannot sign in, send an email from your registered email address. Include “Account deletion request” in the subject and the email address linked to your Skillvaya account. Do not include your password, one-time PIN, full card number or bank credentials.

Email Skillvaya to request deletion

What happens next?

  1. Skillvaya verifies that the request comes from the account holder or an authorised representative.
  2. We review the account and related records so that linked provider listings, messages, attachments, job requests and other personal information are handled appropriately.
  3. We delete or de-identify personal information that no longer needs to be retained, subject to legal obligations, payment/accounting records, fraud prevention, security, active disputes and the rights of other users.
  4. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days. If a request is complex or a lawful retention requirement applies, we will explain the delay and what limited records must be kept.
  5. We contact you when the outcome is confirmed. Sending a request alone does not mean the account has already been deleted.

If you have a paid provider plan, requesting account deletion does not automatically issue a refund or cancel an independent agreement with a service provider. Billing and refund questions can be included in your request.

Need help?

Email skillvaya@gmail.com. For your privacy, do not send passwords or payment-card security details.
